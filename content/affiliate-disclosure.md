Burn.Blue sometimes links to products, tools, books, hardware, software, and services that may pay us a commission if you buy through those links.

As an Amazon Associate, Burn.Blue earns from qualifying purchases.

That means some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, Burn.Blue may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. Tiny money goblin, yes. Secret tracking gremlin, no: the point is to support useful writing, testing, and practical guides without turning the site into an ad swamp.

## How recommendations work

Burn.Blue writes about websites, AI assistants, automation, and the gear that helps small teams work better. Product links may appear in buyer guides, blog posts, setup checklists, tutorials, and resource pages.

Recommendations are based on practical fit, specs, use case, research, known constraints, and/or direct experience when we have it. If a product has not been personally tested, we try to make that clear instead of cosplaying as a lab coat with a ring light.

## What affiliate links do not mean

Affiliate links do not mean:

- a product is automatically the best choice for everyone;
- Burn.Blue guarantees pricing, availability, shipping, warranty, or support;
- the manufacturer reviewed or approved the article;
- you should buy hardware you do not need because the marketing says “AI” in a shiny font.

Prices and availability can change quickly, so check the seller page before buying. Burn.Blue generally avoids hardcoding product prices unless they are served through approved tools or are clearly framed as examples at the time of writing.

## Our bias, stated plainly

Burn.Blue likes useful, boring-on-purpose systems: enough RAM, enough storage, sane backups, clear workflows, and gear that solves an actual bottleneck.

If a cheaper setup does the job, we will say so. If the fancy AI computer is mostly a raccoon in a blazer, we will also say so.
