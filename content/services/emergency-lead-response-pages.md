## A focused page for urgent quote requests

Emergency and time-sensitive service businesses often lose opportunities for simple reasons: the website is unclear, the quote form is buried, the inbox is messy, or the first reply takes too long.

Burn.Blue builds a small lead-response system around one clear page:

- a focused emergency or urgent-service quote page
- plain-language service-area and next-step copy
- a short form or email path that asks for the right details
- routing rules so new requests are easier to notice
- reply templates for common situations
- basic reporting on inquiries received and follow-up status
- optional automation after the current phone, email, booking, or CRM tools are reviewed

The goal is not to replace judgment or dispatch. The goal is to make urgent inquiries easier to understand and respond to before they get lost.

### Good fit

This is most useful for businesses where the customer usually contacts several providers and response time matters, such as:

- tree service and storm cleanup
- garage door repair
- water damage and restoration companies
- pest control and wildlife removal
- appliance, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical repair
- other local services with urgent quote requests

### What the first version includes

Burn.Blue starts with a practical, low-risk setup:

1. clarify the service, area, and situations the business can actually handle
2. build or improve the quote-request page
3. ask for the minimum useful lead details: location, issue, timing, photos if appropriate, and safe contact information
4. route the inquiry to the right inbox
5. prepare response templates that do not overpromise availability, safety, price, licensing, insurance, or response time
6. review what happened after the first leads come through
