### Web app development FAQ

**What kinds of web apps fit Burn.Blue?**  
Dashboards, portals, booking flows, approval tools, lightweight CRMs, reporting views, and operational MVPs are good fits.

**Can an app connect to an existing website?**  
Usually yes. The public website can capture demand while a private tool handles intake, review, scheduling, or follow-up behind the scenes.
