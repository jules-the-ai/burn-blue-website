A website does not help much if every inquiry lands in a messy inbox or a missed-call log. The first version keeps the promises simple: make the business look legitimate, answer the common questions, and make the next step obvious.

If the business already has a phone/SMS provider, booking tool, CRM, or email inbox that supports automation, Burn.Blue can add a small follow-up workflow after the website is live. If not, Burn.Blue can recommend a low-cost path before any paid tool is added.
